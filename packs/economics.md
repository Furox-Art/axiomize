# Economics Pack

Curated pointers for economics/business modeling sessions.

## Archetypes that dominate here
Cournot/Bertrand oligopoly · Hotelling location · Nash bargaining · newsvendor/(s,Q) · Little's Law · Bass diffusion · gravity models (trade/migration)

## Lens priorities
1. Game theory (rivals anticipate)
2. Optimization (budgets, pricing)
3. Stochastic (demand risk)
4. Causal inference (policy/price effect claims from observational sales data)

## Examples to imitate
[café pricing war](../examples/cafe-pricing-war.md) · [supply-chain inventory](../examples/supply-chain-inventory.md) · [startup growth](../examples/startup-growth.md)

## Domain gotchas (feed into Phase 4 as candidate assumptions)
- Elasticities are regime-dependent: a price cut test in one quarter rarely transfers
- Competitor reaction is the rule, not the exception — naive optimization misleads (see café example)
- Revenue ≠ cash flow timing; horizon choice changes sign of recommendations
- Aggregation hides segment structure — check for two-population mixtures before trusting one demand curve

## Typical falsifiers
Price experiment outcomes contradicting fitted elasticity sign; market-share stability where model predicts churn.
